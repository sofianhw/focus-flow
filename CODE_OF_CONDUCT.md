# Code of Conduct (draft)

This draft describes the behavior expected in Focus Flow spaces. It is not a
final enforcement policy until a maintainer confirms an enforcement owner and
a private contact channel.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing code and product decisions.
- Focus feedback on the work, with specific and actionable reasoning.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal or security-sensitive material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
attention without consent, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue, pull request, or discussion. No
enforcement contact is configured yet. Before publication, a maintainer must
add a private reporting channel, name the person or team responsible for
enforcement, and document the review and appeal process. Until those details
are approved, this file remains a draft rather than a claim of an active
moderation process.
